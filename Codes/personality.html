My Top 3 Personality Traits and Hobbies

1. Level Headed

Being level headed as a growing gentleman is one of the best traits to have as it can relate to various aspects of my life and having a positive outlook on most of my endeavors. As it it relates to the impact on my emotions, it aids in thinking before I speak. Added to that, when it comes to my academic life, this traits helps me to have a calm demeanor when the stress of school engulfs me when assignments are due as well as studying.

2. Humor and Charisma

This trait has helped me in many aspects of my life. This includes but not limited to laughing to break a sour mood, making jokes to form closer bonds with family and friends and among other things. As it relates to my academic life, the friendly relationships formed, networking skills and cooperation that stems from this trait has assisted with completing group assignments and opened opportunities that a far more stoic behaviour would limit.

3. Coding and Programming

Coding is considered to be one of my most enjoyable hobbies to do within and beyond my leisure time. Coding would coincide with my academic life, as I am pursuing a degree in Applied AI so it is fitting that this is my hobby. Added to that, it provides a substantial amount of assurance when it comes to my school work and any assignments given as this is my passion

Jurgen Klopp

One of the greatest and most successful footballing manaers once said "If everything is good, perfect. But if not, patience. This quote reminds me that every setback is a learning opportunity and the patience in between is necessary for what is to come. When times are rough, stressing does nothing. LLMs: Tools, Not Crutches

The statement “LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch”. As someone studing Applied AI, it would be foolish to neglect the use of LLMs in my work. I would be asking to be left behind in a society that waits for no one. However, to think that AI should be ued to do work or you is also naive. LLMs are trained and taught on the works done on the internet. Other people, other institutions. All its iformation is someone else's product. To use AI as a crutch kills your creativity, your unique values and your own outstanding character.

When uing AI, I often ask it to expound on my own ideas; helping me to envision what may have been vague and to help wwith mapping out milestones in my work. This allows me to remain efficient and meaningful with my work, while not losing my own values to a machine that I will train in the future. This way, I cannot lose integrity, neither personally, nor academically.
